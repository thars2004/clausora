# Clausora — Hackathon Demo Video Script (< 3 Minutes)

**Target Duration**: 2 minutes 50 seconds  
**Narrator**: Professional, confident, clear  
**Resolution**: 1080p / 60fps  

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & Introduction
- **Visual**: Screen opens on the Clausora landing page. Clean, dark aesthetic with the brand tagline: *"Understand the work. Verify the outcome. Release the payment."*
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "Freelance and client agency projects frequently begin with scattered briefs and vague instructions. Clients and providers often misunderstand deliverable scope, proof of work is detached from acceptance criteria, and releasing payment remains a stressful manual hurdle.
  > Meet **Clausora**: the AI work intelligence and milestone payment orchestration platform built for the PayPal AI Hackathon."

---

### [0:25 - 0:55] Work DNA Engine (Qwen2.5-VL Multimodal Analysis)
- **Visual**: Navigate to **New Work DNA**. Click *"Auto-fill Sample Brief"*. Click *"Synthesize Structured Work Blueprint"*.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "Instead of just generating generic legal jargon, Clausora's **Work DNA Engine**—powered by Hugging Face's multimodal model **Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct**—analyzes briefs, documents, UI screenshots, or even reverse-engineered REA inspection traces.
  > Within seconds, it structures the raw input into workstreams, verifiable tasks, strict acceptance criteria, risk disclosures, and suggested payment milestones."

---

### [0:55 - 1:20] Agreement Snapshot & Cryptographic Fingerprinting
- **Visual**: Click *"Baseline Formal Agreement (SHA-256)"*. Show the generated agreement card with the green verified badge and SHA-256 hash.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "Before any work begins, both parties approve the contract snapshot. Clausora computes an immutable **SHA-256 canonical fingerprint**. This ensures that contract terms, deliverables, and budgets can never be secretly modified."

---

### [1:20 - 1:55] Evidence Graph & AI Advisory Review
- **Visual**: Open the **Evidence Graph** tab for Milestone 2. Click between criteria nodes. Show attached UI screenshots and code links. Highlight the *"Verification Limitation Notice"*.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "As work progresses, the provider submits evidence. Clausora's **Evidence Graph** links proof artifacts directly to specific acceptance criteria.
  > Crucially, our AI engine remains strictly **advisory**. It highlights observed proof, flags missing edge cases, and provides explicit limitation notices—reminding the client that a screenshot alone does not guarantee backend uptime."

---

### [1:55 - 2:20] Scope Drift Radar
- **Visual**: Switch to the **Scope Drift Radar** tab. In the test box, type: *"Can you also add multi-language support (Spanish and French) with an instant currency converter?"* Click *"Scan Scope"*.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "When new client requests arrive mid-project, the **Scope Drift Radar** compares the request with the frozen agreement hash. Here, it detects that multi-language support is an unbudgeted feature change, calculates cost and time impact, and proposes a formal amendment instead of silent scope creep."

---

### [2:20 - 2:45] Real PayPal Sandbox Orders v2 Checkout & Server Capture
- **Visual**: Click *"Approve Milestone for Payment"*. The **PayPal Sandbox Modal** appears. Click *"Initialize PayPal Sandbox Order"*, show the order ID. Then click *"Verify & Capture Payment"*. Show the green verified celebration and the milestone status change to `PAID`.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "Only an explicit client approval triggers payment eligibility. Clausora creates an order using **PayPal Orders v2 Sandbox API** with exact minor currency units.
  > Upon sandbox approval, our backend performs a direct server-to-server capture verification. Only when PayPal confirms completion does the milestone transition to PAID."

---

### [2:45 - 2:55] Audit Timeline & Closing
- **Visual**: Switch to the **Audit Timeline** tab, showing the chronological log with capture references and timestamps.
- **Audio / Voiceover**:
  > "Every agreement hash, evidence submission, review, and PayPal capture reference is permanently preserved in our tamper-evident audit timeline.
  > Clausora connects the brief, the proof, and the payment."
